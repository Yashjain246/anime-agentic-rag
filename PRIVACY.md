# Privacy Policy — Anime RAG Bot

This app ("Anime RAG Bot") is a personal anime & manga chatbot project. This
page explains what data it stores and why.

## What is stored

- **Chat messages** — the questions you ask and the bot's replies, so a
  conversation can continue across turns.
- **An anonymous browser identity** — a random ID generated for your browser
  session, used only to group your chat history together. It is not linked
  to your name, email, or any account.
- **Your current anime/persona/spoiler settings** — e.g. which anime you're
  discussing and what chapter you're up to, so spoilers can be filtered.
- **Optional feedback** — if you submit a rating or comment through the
  feedback form, that text is stored.

No account creation, sign-in, or personal information (name, email, etc.) is
collected or required to use this app.

## What is NOT stored

- This app does not access, read, or store anything from your own Google
  account, calendar, or any other personal service.
- Screenshots you upload for anime identification (via trace.moe) are sent
  to that third-party service for lookup and are not permanently stored by
  this app.

## Google Calendar integration

This app can add anime episode air-date reminders to **a single calendar
owned by the app's developer** (not your calendar). If you use this feature,
you're given a link to that event, which you may optionally add to your own
calendar. The app never requests access to your Google account or calendar.

## Third-party services this app talks to

To answer questions, this app may send your message text to:
- Google Gemini (to generate responses)
- Tavily (to search for anime news)
- trace.moe, OMDB, and MyAnimeList/Jikan (for anime lookups, screenshots, and ratings)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle
data sent to them.

## Data retention & deletion

Chat history is stored so conversations persist across visits. If you'd
like your data deleted, contact the developer at the email below.
